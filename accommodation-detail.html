About this Resort

Luxury resort with stunning views of Petra's ancient city, featuring world-class amenities and authentic Jordanian hospitality. Located at the entrance to the historic site, it's the perfect base for exploring one of the New Seven Wonders of the World.

The resort blends traditional Middle Eastern architecture with modern comfort, offering multiple restaurants, a rooftop garden, and a swimming pool to relax in after a day of exploration.
